# 🌱 AgroWise – Smart Agriculture Assistance Platform

AgroWise is a smart agriculture-focused platform designed to empower farmers with **digital awareness, real-time insights, and decision-making support**.  
The project aims to bridge the gap between **traditional farming practices and modern technology**.

---

## 🚜 Problem Statement
Many farmers still face challenges such as:
- Lack of awareness about government schemes
- Unpredictable weather conditions
- Limited knowledge of crop health and modern techniques
- Low productivity due to outdated practices

AgroWise addresses these issues by providing a **single digital solution**.

---

## 💡 Solution – What AgroWise Does
AgroWise helps farmers by offering:
- Awareness about **government schemes**
- **Weather updates** for better crop planning
- **Crop health insights**
- Easy-to-understand agricultural information
- Data-driven impact analysis using graphs

---

## ✨ Key Features
- 🌾 Farmer Awareness Dashboard  
- 🌦️ Weather & Seasonal Updates  
- 🧑‍🌾 Government Schemes Information  
- 🌱 Crop Health & Productivity Insights  
- 📊 Before vs After Impact Analysis  
- 📈 Bar Graphs & Pie Charts for visualization
